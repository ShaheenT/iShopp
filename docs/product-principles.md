# iShopp Product Principles

## Product promise

Share useful savings. Discover genuine specials. Make better shopping decisions.

## Source of truth

PostgreSQL/Supabase is authoritative for application and transactional state.

## AI boundary

AI can extract, classify, normalize and suggest. AI cannot authorize payments, alter financial truth, or bypass application authorization.

## Backend-first delivery

Every backend capability must include its data model/migration, authorization/RLS, implementation, API contract and automated verification before frontend polish.

## Legacy prototype

`ShaheenT/iShoppTest` is reference material only. It contains useful concepts such as deals, retailers/stores, categories, social sharing, likes/comments and shopping lists, but its Vite/Express/Drizzle architecture is not carried into production unchanged.
